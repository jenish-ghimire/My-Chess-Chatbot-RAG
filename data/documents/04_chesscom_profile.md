## Platform Comparison Note

Chess.com uses its own internal rating system (similar to Elo but proprietary). Ratings between Chess.com, Lichess, and FIDE are **not directly comparable** because each platform uses a different system and has a different player pool. Generally:

- **Lichess** ratings tend to be the highest of the three
- **Chess.com** ratings tend to fall between FIDE and Lichess
- **FIDE** Elo ratings tend to be the lowest of the three for the same player

This does not reflect actual skill differences — it is simply a result of different calibration systems.
