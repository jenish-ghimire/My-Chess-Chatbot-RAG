**Q: What is the difference between FIDE Elo and Lichess ratings?**
A: FIDE uses the classic Elo rating system for over-the-board tournament games. Lichess uses Glicko-2, which is a more modern system designed to better handle inactive players and uncertainty. As a result, Lichess ratings are typically 200–400 points higher than FIDE equivalents. Chess.com uses its own proprietary system. None of these is "more real" than the others — they each measure performance within their own ecosystem.
